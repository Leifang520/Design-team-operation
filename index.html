Goal Setting
Career growth inside Delivery Hero — performance conversations, growth goals, promotion clarity.

What we're already doing

  • Individual growth plans with DH templates; cadence agreed between manager & designer
  • Expectation conversations covering role, half-year targets, quarterly focus
  • Team OKRs — Design, Research, Deliveries (Rider team goal by Momo in progress)
  • Career frameworks in flight: AI designer (Rona + leadership), UXW (Rona), Design Systems (Enda/Murat/Lucas — needs follow-up)

Gap Analysis — What we're doing vs. What the team needs

What we're doing
  • Growth plans exist but rely on each designer to drive them forward
  • Expectation conversations happen at cycle start, rarely revisited mid-cycle
  • OKRs set at team level, not always connected to individual career moves
  • Career frameworks are being built but not yet shared or actionable
  • 1:1s exist but focus on project status, not performance or growth
What the team needs
  • Regular mid-cycle check-ins so goals stay relevant — no EOY surprises
  • Transparent, written criteria for what "exceeding expectations" and promotion look like
  • A clear skill tree showing current placement and next steps
  • Goals re-aligned when business context shifts, not left to go stale
  • A way to submit documented evidence of impact before review — not just manager perception

Gap Analysis — What we're doing vs. What the team needs

What we're doing
  • Educational budget exists but uptake is left entirely to individuals
  • Weekly sharing sessions run but attendance is uneven and optional
  • AI learning is available but approach is fragmented — every person figuring it out alone
  • Mentorship program is piloting — no scale or structured outcomes yet
  • Growth sits in domain silos — no team-wide strategy or shared accountability
What the team needs
  • Protected, team-wide learning time — not "whatever is left after delivery"
  • Shared AI guidelines so adoption is consistent, not chaotic
  • Learning treated as a team OKR, not an individual responsibility
  • Mandatory rituals (crits, showcases) with equal participation expected
  • Cross-squad exposure — pairing and feedback beyond their own workstream
  • A clear path from growth-in-practice to recognition and advancement
